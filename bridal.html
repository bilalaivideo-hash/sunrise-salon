How the booking works

Four steps, in this order.

01

Hold the date

Send your wedding dates on WhatsApp. We confirm availability and take an advance to hold them.

02

Pre-bridal course

Optional facials, polishing and hair treatment in the weeks before, so your skin is ready.

03

Trial session

We build the look on your face with your jewellery and dupatta, photograph it, and adjust.

04

The day itself

Your artist works to the agreed timeline, in salon or at home, and hands you a touch-up kit.
