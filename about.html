Our story

Built slowly, on repeat clients.

Abeeha started with makeup — a handful of brides, a kit carried in a bag, appointments taken on a phone that never stopped ringing. Word travelled the way it does here: through mothers, sisters and cousins who had seen the photographs.

The salon came next, and then the rooms behind it: a facial room with proper machines, a colour bar, a nail station, a curtained massage suite. Each one added only when we had a stylist good enough to run it.

Today Sun Rise is one of the largest ladies salons in the district, with more than nine thousand people following our work online and a Google rating we protect carefully. But the measure that matters is simpler — most of the women who walk in this week have been here before.

What we promise

Standards we do not move on.

Tools are sterilised, not wiped

Metal tools go through a steriliser between clients. Files, buffers and wax strips are used once and thrown away in front of you.

Products stay sealed until used

We buy branded professional lines and open them at the station. If you want to see the tube, ask — we will show you.

The salon is closed to men

Female staff only, curtained treatment rooms, and no photography without your permission. Your privacy is the service.

You get told the truth

If a treatment will not suit your hair or skin, we say so and suggest something that will, even when it costs less.

The price is agreed first

Length, condition and product all affect cost, so we quote before we start. The bill matches the quote.

Your time is kept

Appointments are spaced so you are not left waiting behind someone else's bridal trial.
