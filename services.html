Before you arrive

Three things worth knowing.

Book ahead on weekends

Friday to Sunday fills quickly, especially in wedding season. Two days' notice is usually enough; bridal needs three weeks.

Tell us about your skin

Allergies, active acne, recent peels or pregnancy all change what we use. It is never a problem — we just need to know.

Arrive with clean skin

For facials and makeup, come without makeup if you can. It saves twenty minutes of your appointment.
